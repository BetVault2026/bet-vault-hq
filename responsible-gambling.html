Responsible Betting – Stay in Control

Enjoy the sport. Know your limits. Protect your future.

At Bet Vault HQ, we believe that gambling, if you choose to participate, should be a form of entertainment, never a source of income or a solution to financial difficulties.

Understand Winning and Losing

No bet is guaranteed to win. Always be prepared to lose your entire stake. Never chase your losses. A losing bet does not mean your next bet will win. Never increase your stakes to recover lost money.

Protect Your Finances

Only gamble with money you can afford to lose. Set a spending limit and stick to it. Never borrow money to place a bet. Never gamble with money intended for rent, bills, food or family expenses. Never use gambling as a way to repay debts.

Keep Gambling in Perspective

Set time limits and take regular breaks. Avoid gambling when stressed, upset or under the influence of alcohol. Never feel pressured to place a bet. Remember that you can enjoy sport without gambling. If gambling starts affecting your finances, relationships or wellbeing, stop and seek support.

Recognise the Warning Signs

If you find yourself hiding losses, borrowing money, struggling to stop or constantly thinking about your next bet, it may be time to seek help.

Your health, family and financial security are always more important than any bet.
