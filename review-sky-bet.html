Sky Bet Review UK – Mobile App, RequestABet & Features

Overview

Sky Bet offers one of the cleanest, most accessible mobile betting experiences in the UK market. Particularly popular among football fans, the platform stands out for its RequestABet custom market builder, regular price boosts, and straightforward navigation across desktop, iOS, and Android.

Key Strengths

  • Clean, beginner-friendly mobile app with intuitive navigation.
  • Strong coverage of English football leagues, including Championship and League One/Two markets.
  • Minimum deposit varies by payment method; check current operator terms.

Limitations
