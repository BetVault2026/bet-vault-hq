Privacy & Cookies

Who We Are

Bet Vault HQ is operated by Bet Vault in London, United Kingdom, and is responsible for the personal information it collects. Contact: admin@betvaulthq.co.uk. This notice covers Bet Vault HQ (betvaulthq.co.uk), including membership registration and voluntary contributions.

Data We Collect

If you contact us by email, we receive your address and message to respond. Membership submissions include your name, email, optional mobile number and message, age confirmation and privacy acknowledgement. We use this information to administer membership and respond to enquiries. We do not automatically subscribe members to marketing emails.

We use personal information where necessary to respond to requests, administer the website and protect its security, relying on applicable lawful bases including legitimate interests, contractual necessity where applicable, and consent where required.

Third-Party Processors and Services

Cloudflare hosts the website and may process technical request information for delivery and security. FormSubmit processes membership submissions and forwards them to our email address. Our email service providers handle correspondence. Stripe processes voluntary contributions on its own payment page; we do not collect card details on this website. External websites are subject to their own privacy notices.

Cookies & Affiliate Tracking

Essential technical cookies, including Cloudflare security cookies where applicable, may be used to keep the site secure and functioning. When commercial affiliate links are active, clicking an operator link may lead to referral tracking by an affiliate network or bookmaker to attribute the visit. We do not collect your bookmaker payment card details. Third-party tracking is subject to the relevant provider’s privacy and cookie notices.

Where we introduce non-essential cookies or similar tracking technologies that require consent, we will request consent before using them and provide a way to withdraw it. See our Affiliate Disclosure for commercial link information.

Data Retention

We aim to retain enquiry and membership records only for as long as necessary to respond to enquiries, administer membership or provide requested services. Retention may also be necessary to meet legal obligations or resolve disputes. You may request deletion, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Your UK GDPR Rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may have rights to access, correct or erase your personal information, restrict or object to processing, receive portable data and withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Contact admin@betvaulthq.co.uk to exercise your rights. You may also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
