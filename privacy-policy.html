Privacy policy

Publisher: Bet Vault. Contact: betvault2026@protonmail.com.

This notice explains the intended handling of personal information when you use Bet Vault. The site provides sports information, bookmaker information and educational material. It is not a bookmaker and does not operate gambling accounts.

Information you provide

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and the information you include so that we can respond and keep a record of the enquiry. Please do not send payment-card details or sensitive personal information by email.

Where a contact or membership form powered by FormSubmit is enabled, the information entered into that form is transmitted to FormSubmit for delivery to our contact inbox. FormSubmit may process technical and submission data as described in its own privacy information. Only submit information needed for your enquiry.

Payments

If a voluntary contribution is offered through Stripe, payment details are entered into Stripe's payment interface and processed by Stripe. Bet Vault does not intend to receive or store full payment-card numbers. Stripe's privacy terms apply to its processing.

Hosting, logs and sports data

The website is hosted using Cloudflare. Cloudflare may process technical information, including IP address, browser and request data, for delivery, security and reliability. Sports information may be retrieved from third-party data providers; we do not ask visitors to provide personal information to view ordinary public pages.

Cookies and similar technologies

We do not intentionally deploy analytics or advertising cookies in this site version. Cloudflare and other service providers may use strictly necessary technologies for security and delivery. If non-essential analytics, advertising or affiliate tracking is introduced, this notice and the cookie controls will be updated before those technologies are used where consent is required.

Use, sharing and retention

We use enquiry information to respond, maintain appropriate records and protect the site. We do not sell personal information. Information may be handled by the service providers described above or disclosed where legally required. We retain enquiries only for as long as reasonably needed for their purpose, legal obligations and dispute handling, then securely delete or anonymise them.

Your rights

Depending on your circumstances, UK data protection law may give you rights to access, correct, erase or restrict use of your personal information, object to certain processing and complain to the Information Commissioner's Office. Contact us at the email above to make a request. You can also contact the ICO at ico.org.uk.
