Decimal odds

Decimal odds show total potential return including stake. Odds of 2.00 mean a £10 stake could return £20 in total if successful.

②

Accumulator bets

An accumulator combines selections: all selections generally need to win for the bet to succeed, subject to market rules. Risk increases with each selection.

③

Betting terminology

Stake: money risked. Return: total paid back on a successful bet. Profit: return minus stake. Always read market rules.

Beginner’s Guide

A bet risks money on an uncertain outcome. You can lose the full stake. Before considering a bet, understand the market, odds and settlement rules. Gambling is optional and should never be treated as a source of income.

Understanding Odds

Decimal odds (e.g., 2.50) include your stake in the return, while UK fractional odds (e.g., 6/4) show profit relative to your stake. Both represent the exact same payout—a £10 winning bet returns £25 in total (£15 profit).

Types of Bets

Singles cover one outcome, doubles combine two, and accumulators require all legs to win, subject to settlement rules. For horse racing and golf, Each-Way bets split your stake equally into a Win bet and a Place bet.

Understanding Free Bets

Promotional credits have conditions and are not usually withdrawable cash. With stake-not-returned offers, only the profit from a successful free bet is paid out. Check minimum odds, expiry dates and qualifying requirements.

Managing Your Money

Set a strict monthly betting budget you can afford to lose and keep individual stakes within that limit. No staking percentage guarantees safety or profit. Never increase stakes to chase previous losses, or borrow to gamble.

Responsible Gambling

Gambling should never interfere with your finances, relationships or wellbeing. Deposit limits, time-outs and self-exclusion can help you control or stop gambling. Recognise warning signs and find support.
