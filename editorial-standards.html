Editorial Standards

Our approach

We aim to publish original, dated content, verify factual claims, correct errors and check bookmaker licences. Commercial relationships must not determine editorial ratings.

How We Rate Bookmakers

Published editorial ratings are independent assessments. Our review framework uses the following weighted criteria; full documented verification of the current scores is pending. We aim to review ratings regularly to reflect changes in licence status, payment options and safer gambling tools.

  • Safer Gambling Tools (30%): Availability of deposit limits, reality checks, time-outs, self-exclusion and GAMSTOP participation.
  • Payment Transparency (20%): Clarity of deposit and withdrawal methods, limits, fees and processing times.
  • Customer Support (15%): Availability of contact channels, published support hours and clarity of assistance.
  • Usability (15%): Ease of navigation, accessibility and clarity of desktop and mobile interfaces.
  • Sports Coverage (10%): Breadth of sports, competitions and available market information.
  • Clarity of Terms (10%): Accessibility and clarity of account rules, promotional conditions and settlement terms.

UK Gambling Commission licence status is a separate eligibility check, not a substitute for a documented rating assessment.

Fact-Checking & Corrections Policy

Our policy is to check UKGC licence numbers against the Gambling Commission public register and verify time-sensitive odds and promotional terms against operator sources before presenting them as verified. When reporting an error, please include the page address, the information in question and any supporting source. We welcome error reports at admin@betvaulthq.co.uk and aim to investigate and correct inaccuracies promptly.
